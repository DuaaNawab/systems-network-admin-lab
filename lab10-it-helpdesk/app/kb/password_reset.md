# Password reset
1. Open the university student portal login page and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter your student ID and the registered email address.
3. Open the reset link sent to your email (valid for 30 minutes) and choose a new password of at least 10 characters.
4. If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check the spam folder, then contact the IT helpdesk.
Staff never ask for your password. Do not share it with anyone.
