# Account access
1. An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes.
2. Check that Caps Lock is off and that you are using your student ID, not your email, on the portal.
3. If access to a course or the LMS is missing, confirm that you are enrolled in that course for this semester.
4. New students receive access within 24 hours of fee confirmation.
5. If you still cannot sign in, the helpdesk must verify your identity before changing anything on your account.
